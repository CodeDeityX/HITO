# Code of Conduct

HITO welcomes technical disagreement, critical review, and adversarial
testing.

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally and focus criticism
on ideas, evidence, implementation, and behavior rather than personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate doxxing, and intentional
disruption of project collaboration are not acceptable.

Good-faith disagreement with a maintainer, architecture decision, benchmark
result, or project claim is not itself misconduct.
