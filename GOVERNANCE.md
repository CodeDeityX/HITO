# HITO Governance

HITO was founded and originally authored by **CodeDeityX**.

Canonical public repository:

https://github.com/CodeDeityX/HITO

HITO currently uses a maintainer-led open-source model.

Anyone may inspect, use, fork, open issues, and propose changes under the
project license. Changes become canonical only through explicit maintainer
acceptance into the canonical repository.

Community contribution does not erase the historical origin of HITO.

Founder / Original Author: **CodeDeityX**

The governance model may evolve publicly as the contributor community grows.
